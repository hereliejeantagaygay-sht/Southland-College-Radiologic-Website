About Our Department

The Radiologic Technology Department focuses on educating students in the science and practice of medical imaging. Students develop the knowledge, technical skills, and professional values needed in the healthcare field.

Our Mission

To provide quality education and training that develop competent, ethical, and compassionate future radiologic technologists.

Our Vision

To develop responsible healthcare professionals who contribute to quality patient care and medical imaging services.
